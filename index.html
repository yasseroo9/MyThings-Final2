EXPERIENCE WITH BI

A Bachelor's degree project applying Business Intelligence to a real Jordanian e-food platform.

BACKGROUND

The rapid growth of digital platforms has significantly increased the importance of data in improving operational performance. E-Food platforms rely heavily on customer service operations, order management, and real-time support to ensure satisfaction.

MyThings is a Jordanian E-Food platform. As users and orders increase, managing customer interactions and service performance becomes more complex — requiring BI tools to surface actionable insights.

OBJECTIVES

Data Collection
Collect and organize operational data related to customer service performance.
KPI Analysis vs Targets
Analyze CSAT, DSAT, AHT, ART, FRT, FCR against defined performance benchmarks.
AI Feedback Intelligence
Classify customer feedback as positive or negative and generate actionable improvement suggestions.
FTE Workforce Planning
Calculate required agents per hour from chat volume and AHT to support staffing decisions.
Peak Hour Detection
Identify high-traffic hours and flag them for proactive resource allocation.

PERFORMANCE TARGETS

CSAT Target → 65%+ positive (≥4/5)
At least 65% of interactions should receive a CSAT score of 4 or 5 out of 5.
FRT Target → 0:00:30 (30 seconds)
First response should be delivered within 30 seconds of customer contact.
AHT Target → 0:15:00 (15 minutes)
Average handling time should not exceed 15 minutes per customer interaction.
ART Target → 0:00:45 (45 seconds)
Average agent response time between replies should stay under 45 seconds.

FTE CALCULATOR

AI-calculated staffing requirements per hour based on daily chat volume and a 15-minute AHT target at 85% occupancy.

STAFFING BY HOUR

Formula: FTEs = (Avg chats/hour × AHT in hours) ÷ Occupancy rate
= (Chats × 0.25) ÷ 0.85  |  AHT = 15 min · Occupancy = 85%
